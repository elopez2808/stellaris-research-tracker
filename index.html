New here?How to use it+
  1. Pick an area. Tap Physics, Society or Engineering, or drag the slider between them.
  2. Tick off what you've researched. Tap a tech card to mark it done; tap again to undo. Starting a new game? Press Mark starting techs first.
  3. Describe your empire. In Your empire, set the year, planets, starbases, authority, ethics, traditions, ascension perks, civics, origin and the DLC and mods you play with. Every card's odds update as you go.
  4. Read the odds. The big % on each card is the chance that tech shows up among your next research options in that area. Boosted and Reduced list the modifiers working on it now; Could boost shows what would raise it.
  5. Watch the tiers. A tier opens once you've researched enough techs in the tier below (6 in vanilla; ACOT's tiers 6 to 10 need 2 or 4). Finished tiers grey out, and techs you can't be offered yet are greyed with the reason.
  6. Filter. Use search and the switches at the top, or the pills on each tier, to see only what's in the draw, what's left, or what you've done.
  7. Keep your progress. Use + for a new campaign. If you're signed in to Claude your progress is saved to your account; otherwise it stays in this browser. To move it to another device, use Copy backup code in the ≡ menu.

Odds are estimates built from the game's own research weights. Events, special projects, debris and a few rare situations aren't modelled, and other mods you run can change things.
